    Data accuracy audit and pipeline repair (October 2026)¶

    What was checked, what was wrong, what was changed, and what is still open. Every number below was measured on the files in this repository.

    1. Village count: 954 vs 951¶

    The polygons are 954. The dashboard served 951 histories because village_timeseries.json, village_baselines.json and villages_by_tehsil.json were keyed by tehsil::village-name, and three pairs of villages share a name inside one tehsil:

    Tehsil Name Polygon ids
    Ahmadpur Anandwadi 4, 5
    Chakur Hatkarwadi 293, 294
    Nilanga Kamshetwadi 642, 643

    One polygon of each pair was drawn with its namesake's numbers. Fixed by keying everything on the polygon id: scripts/analysis/build_village_timeseries.py, scripts/analysis/build_village_registry.py, the map, sidebar, drawer, timeseries page and /api/v1/timeseries?id=. The dropdown selects by id, so two "Anandwadi" entries are two selectable villages. scripts/tests/test_pipeline.py fails if the count ever drifts again.

    2. Legends that did not match the map¶

    • The map painted CDI with the US-DM palette (None = white), while the legend swatches were green / yellow / amber. SPI polygons used cut-offs (-0.5, -1.0, -1.5, -2.0) that disagree with classify.py (-0.5, -0.8, -1.3, -1.6, -2.0).
    • NDVI, VCI, VHI and rainfall layers showed the CDI legend ("None, D0 ... D4").
    • The compare and timelapse pages classified every index with the SPI function, so an NDVI of 0.3 was coloured as "no drought".
    • The sidebar re-derived the drought class from 0.2 / 0.4 / 0.6 / 0.8 instead of 0.20 / 0.35 / 0.50 / 0.65 / 0.80, so the sidebar and the map could disagree on the same village.

    Now frontend/lib/metrics.ts is the only place a layer's bins, colours and labels are defined; the fill colour, legend, tooltip, compare/timelapse pages and sidebar all read it. The legend also shows how many villages fall in each bin for the month on screen and says so when a layer has no data. Tests compare the TypeScript palette and thresholds against classify.py and config/composite.yaml. The "no drought" colour changed from white to sage because white was invisible on a light basemap.

    3. Baseline drought maps¶

    village_baselines.json existed but nothing mapped it. New layers, group "Baseline": normal NDVI for the month, normal rainfall for the month, NDVI departure (z-score), rainfall as a percentage of normal. Definitions follow baseline_drought_architecture.md section 2 (per-village, per-calendar-month mean and standard deviation over 2016-2026). The legend's "defn" button states each layer's definition.

    4. Are the backend indices right for each village?¶

    Check Result
    SPI-1/3/6/12 recomputed from the village rain table (Thom gamma, Stagge zero-centre) vs stored SPI correlation 0.9999 to 1.0000, mean abs. difference 0.002 to 0.006. SPI arithmetic is correct.
    Village CDI recomputed from the stored inputs and config/composite.yaml identical to the stored value (max diff 0.0)
    Village PCI recomputed identical
    Class vs score in the served time series 73 of 75,068 rows disagree, all within rounding of a threshold

    Problems that are real and not fixed here, because fixing them changes published numbers and needs the team's decision (AGENTS.md rule 2):

    1. EVI is wrong at source. The zonal EVI has median exactly 1.0; 20 % of village-months equal 1.0 and 16 % exceed 1.0 (max 1.38). build_baseline_drought.py then silently clips to [-1, 1], hiding it. Its own comment says "DN not scaled by 10000". EVI carries weight 0.10 in the CDI. The dashboard no longer offers EVI as a map layer; the real-time job withholds EVI from new months. Rebuild EVI from the rasters with the reflectance scale fixed.
    2. VHI equals VCI in 100 % of rows (no thermal band, documented in build_village_indices.py). With weights VHI 0.20 + VCI 0.15 the same signal carries 35 % of the composite, and it makes the min_inputs: 3 rule easy to satisfy. Either drop VHI from the composite or build it as 0.5 VCI + 0.5 PCI as memory.md defines.
    3. Rainfall is sampled at the village centroid on a ~5 km grid. The rain used for PCI has about 263 distinct values across 954 villages in a month (a village's rain is the value of the CHIRPS cell holding its centroid), and the rain used for SPI has about 700. They are two different samplings of the same data. Neighbouring villages therefore share one number and cliffs appear at cell edges. This is the main reason a village's rainfall can look wrong against ground knowledge. A polygon zonal mean computed once from the CHIRPS rasters for both PCI and SPI would fix the inconsistency (it cannot be rebuilt from this repository: the rasters are not committed).
    4. SPI is calibrated on 2016-2026 only (about 10 values per calendar month). The status file used to claim "1991-2020". It now states the real reference. With so few values the gamma fit is rough; values beyond +/-2 deserve caution. Fetching CHIRPS 1991-2020 from Earth Engine and fitting on that is the standard remedy.
    5. The CDI class is sensitive to the short record. Each village-month is judged against the median and MAD of about 7 values. Scoring a month against a reference that excludes the month itself reproduces the stored class for 89-100 % of villages in most months, but only 63 % for October 2026. Treat single-month class flips as uncertain.
    6. Three Sentinel-2 filename conventions are radiometrically inconsistent (corrected with a per-batch additive offset, estimate_offsets in build_village_indices.py). New months come from Earth Engine S2_SR_HARMONIZED, a different processing chain; a bias check against the historic NDVI over an overlapping month should be run on the first live data.
    7. Monsoon gaps. 22 of 98 Sentinel-2 months have a composite for fewer than half of the villages (June to September in most years). The map shows them as "no data" and the legend counts them.
    8. CHIRPS stops at 2026-08. 2026-09 and 2026-10 have no rainfall, SPI or PCI, so their CDI uses only NDVI, VCI and VHI (which equals VCI). The home page shows input completeness for the month.

    5. Real-time pipeline¶

    Why it never worked on GitHub Actions, in the order it fails:

    1. chirps_gee.py and pipeline_gee.py default to data/vector/latur_tehsils.geojson; the folder contains only .gitkeep, so both exit with "AOI not found".
    2. chirps_gee.py called ee.Initialize() with no credentials and fell back to the interactive ee.Authenticate(), which hangs a CI job. It also read UCSB-CHG/CHIRPS/monthly, which Earth Engine does not publish (only DAILY and PENTAD), and tested img is None on a server-side object.
    3. build_village_indices.py needs rasterio and local rasters, neither on the runner. The workflow treated that failure as "expected".
    4. run_realtime_pipeline_sync.py ignored every failed step, never wrote the new month into village_timeseries.json or the village GeoJSON, overwrote latest_status.json with monitoring_status: "operational" and both sources "operational" regardless of outcome, and inserted a hard-coded Supabase row (village "Latur", cloud 5.2 %, SPI-1 copied from SPI-3).
    5. The workflow did not install pyyaml (imported at module load), did not pass the period input, and always printed "SUCCESS".
    6. /api/v1/realtime and /api/v1/realtime/validate returned constants: "operational", "latest period 2026-10", "96.25 % accuracy", "954 boundaries with 0 gaps".
    7. The header showed a pulsing green "Live Sentinel-2 - GEE Operational" at all times.
    8. latest_status.json was not in frontend/public/data, so the status route always fell back to a placeholder.

    What replaced it: scripts/realtime/realtime_update.py (details in its docstring and .github/workflows/realtime_drought_sync.yml).

    • Village-level Sentinel-2 NDVI and CHIRPS rainfall from Earth Engine, month by month, oldest first; a CHIRPS month is used only when every daily image is published.
    • SPI recomputed for all four scales; before writing, the job recomputes SPI on the stored history and aborts if it drifts from the stored values.
    • VCI/VHI/PCI/CDI computed with config/composite.yaml against the existing record; history is not rewritten. Scoring an existing month reproduces its stored score exactly (tested).
    • A month whose Sentinel-2 arrived before its rainfall is re-scored when the rainfall lands, which is exactly the 2026-09 / 2026-10 situation today.
    • Rebuilds the history file, baselines, district series and latest GeoJSON.
    • latest_status.json records what happened. The job exits non-zero when a due month produced nothing; the workflow still commits the status so the site shows "Behind" or "Incomplete" rather than a stale "operational", then fails the run.
    • The status chip, /api/v1/realtime and /api/v1/realtime/validate now read the real files; the validator computes its checks from the served data.

    Not verified here: no Earth Engine credentials were available in this environment, so the two fetch functions (fetch_s2, fetch_chirps) have not been run against the live service. Everything after them was exercised end to end with --fixture (rain for 2026-09 and 2026-10 plus a synthetic 2026-11): 954 villages scored, SPI tables extended, history left identical, status written. Run python scripts/realtime/realtime_update.py --period 2026-09 once with the service account to confirm.

    6. Other fixes¶

    • Upload page posted to /upload/aoi, a route that does not exist in the Next.js app. The AOI is now analysed in the browser (GeoJSON in EPSG:4326): drawn on the map, intersected with the 954 villages, area-weighted CDI, class split, village table, CSV download.
    • Tehsil and district outlines were interactive polygons stacked above the villages and could swallow hover/click events; they are now inert panes. Cursor: open hand on the map, pointer only over a village. Tooltips no longer follow the cursor.
    • Overlaps: basemap picker and control bar shared the same corner; the legend and timeline shared the bottom edge; the village drawer covered the page header. Legend and timeline now sit in one dock, controls in separate slots, the drawer inside the map frame.
    • The Google tile URLs (mt1.google.com) are not an authorised tile API. The default basemap is now a plain CARTO light map; Google entries remain selectable, review before a public launch.
    • Removed the sidebar "Official Government Drought Zone Notice" with fixed counts (490 / 228); it is now a sentence computed from the villages on screen.

    7. Published accuracy figures that cannot be reproduced from this repository¶

    docs/README.md and docs/VALIDATION_AND_ACCURACY_REPORT.md state a hit rate of 94.21 %, false-alarm ratio 5.76 %, CSI 0.8906 and overall accuracy 96.25 %, from "28,450 of 30,200 actual drought crises" matched to "government declarations / relief dispatches". The repository contains no ground-truth dataset and no script that computes these metrics; the only code that mentioned them was the hard-coded /api/v1/realtime/validate response, now removed. The same reports describe 951 villages, a CDI of 0.50 PCI + 0.25 VCI + 0.25 TCI and a TCI band that does not exist in the pipeline (the real composite is in config/composite.yaml). Until the underlying declaration data and the scoring script are added, do not quote these numbers externally.
