    Earth Observation Satellites & Sensors¶

    The Latur drought monitoring platform fuses multi-spectral, microwave radar, thermal infrared, and precipitation sensors to achieve continuous spatio-temporal observation.

    2. Sensor Ingestion Specifications¶

    2.1 Copernicus Sentinel-2 Multi-Spectral Instrument (MSI)¶

    Sentinel-2A (launched June 2015) and Sentinel-2B (launched March 2017) fly in a Sun-synchronous orbit at \(786\text{ km}\) altitude with a combined 5-day revisit period over Latur district (Tile IDs: 43QDB, 43QDC, 43QEB, 43QEC).

    • Bands Ingested:
    • Band 2 (Blue): \(490\text{ nm}\) (\(10\text{ m}\)) — Aerosol correction and EVI computation.
    • Band 4 (Red): \(665\text{ nm}\) (\(10\text{ m}\)) — Maximum chlorophyll absorption.
    • Band 8 (NIR): \(842\text{ nm}\) (\(10\text{ m}\)) — Mesophyll cellular scattering.
    • Scene Classification Layer (SCL): \(20\text{ m}\) — Pixel-level quality flag generated by Sen2Cor (identifies clouds, cloud shadows, cirrus, snow, water, and bare soil).

    2.2 Copernicus Sentinel-1 C-Band Synthetic Aperture Radar (C-SAR)¶

    Sentinel-1 provides all-weather, day-and-night radar imaging, unaffected by dense monsoon convective cloud formations that obstruct optical sensors between June and September. - Operating Mode: Interferometric Wide (IW) Ground Range Detected (GRD). - Polarizations: Dual-polarization Vertical-Transmit / Vertical-Receive (\(\text{VV}\)) and Vertical-Transmit / Horizontal-Receive (\(\text{VH}\)). - Dielectric Sensitivity: The real component of complex soil dielectric permittivity increases exponentially with volumetric water content (\(0.05\text{--}0.45\ \text{cm}^3\cdot\text{cm}^{-3}\)), elevating surface radar backscatter by \(4\text{--}8\text{ dB}\) between dry Vertisol and field saturation.

    2.3 Landsat 8/9 Thermal Infrared Sensor (TIRS)¶

    Landsat 8 (launched February 2013) and Landsat 9 (launched September 2021) carry the two-band Thermal Infrared Sensor (TIRS). - Band 10: \(10.60\text{--}11.19\ \mu\text{m}\) (acquisition resolution \(100\text{ m}\), resampled to \(30\text{ m}\)). - Used to compute Land Surface Temperature (LST) and Temperature Condition Index (TCI), capturing diurnal evaporative cooling anomalies during pre-monsoon and post-monsoon phases.

    2.4 UCSB Climate Hazards Group InfraRed Precipitation with Station Data (CHIRPS v2.0)¶

    CHIRPS blends \(0.05^\circ\) resolution satellite cold cloud duration (CCD) observations with in-situ station rainfall records from global and Indian national meteorological networks. - Provides uninterrupted daily rainfall time series from 1981 to present. - Ingested directly into the rolling SPI engine (SPI-1, 3, 6, 12).
